Efe Clips Naija

A private tool for posting short video clips to one YouTube channel, one Facebook Page and one Instagram account.

Efe Clips Naija is a small personal tool run by a single person. It finds publicly shared Twitch clips of the Nigerian streamer Cater Efe, formats them as vertical videos with credit to the streamer, and posts them to the tool owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account after the owner has had a chance to approve or reject each one. It is an unofficial fan page.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. The only permissions it uses are the permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to publish videos to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account.
